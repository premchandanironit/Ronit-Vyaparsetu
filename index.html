Innovation Presentation & Architecture Deck

VyapaarSetu: Hyperlocal MSME Intelligence Architecture

Engineered for Ministry of MSME / DPIIT Problem Statement: Eliminating information asymmetry between centralized e-commerce giants and 63+ million Indian micro-enterprises.

The Core Problem

Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian retailers rely on supplier guesswork and nationwide trend lists. This leads to over-stocking in saturating goods, inventory depreciation, and capital lockups that destroy early-stage ventures.

Full-Stack C# & Web Architecture

  • Backend API: ASP.NET Core 8 Web API with C# Controllers, Domain Services & DTO records.
  • City Normalization: Normalizing demand curves across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 economic baselines.
  • OpenAPI / Swagger: Complete RESTful API documentation at /docs for jury inspection.
  • Zero-Dependency Client: Fast, accessible vanilla frontend running on any modern browser.

National Scheme Integration

Unlike purely analytical tools, VyapaarSetu matches verified venture opportunities directly to PMEGP 35% capital subsidies, PM Mudra Yojana (Shishu/Kishor/Tarun), and CGTMSE collateral-free credit guarantees.

Alignment with ONDC & Viksit Bharat

Designed to seamlessly plug into ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) buyer-seller discovery APIs, enabling localized supply chain resilience across Bharat.
